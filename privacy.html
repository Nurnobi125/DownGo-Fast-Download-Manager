DownGo Privacy Policy

This policy explains how DownGo handles information when you use the Windows app, browser extension, or website support form.

Publisher: Rafix Technologies · Bangladesh
Privacy and support contact: contact@downgo.online

1. The Windows app

DownGo does not require you to create a DownGo account to use its core download features. Download URLs, filenames, queue and download history, and app preferences are processed by the app and stored locally on your Windows device. The app sends download requests to the file or media hosts represented by the URLs you choose. Those hosts may receive your connection details and any request information required to serve the file; their privacy policies apply.

If you enable browser-session cookie use or import a cookies file, DownGo stores and uses those credentials on your device to make downloads that you request. Cookies can grant access to your signed-in sessions. Only import or enable cookies for accounts and sites you are authorized to use, and remove imported cookies when you no longer need them.

2. Browser extension

The extension can temporarily read links and visible link names when you ask it to scan a page. If the Video Download Panel is enabled, it checks media element URLs and observes media response URLs and content-type metadata on pages where the browser permits it. Auto-Capture and the Video Download Panel are off by default. You can change these controls and the extension's site access in your browser settings.

When you choose a download, or enable Auto-Capture for a matching browser download, the extension sends the selected URL, filename, page referrer, and, when needed, matching cookies to the DownGo app on the same computer through its local connection. This information is used to perform the download you requested. The extension's disclosed download handoff data is not sent to DownGo's website or used for advertising or analytics. It does not collect passwords or browsing history. Tab media candidates are temporary and are removed when the tab closes; extension settings are stored in browser storage.

3. Pro license and activation

Free use does not make a DownGo license-server request. If you choose to activate or manage a paid Pro license, the app sends your license key and device identifier and name to DownGo's license service. The service checks the purchase with Gumroad and uses activation information to validate the license and enforce the plan's device-seat limit. Device identifiers and license keys are hashed for license-server records; the service may retain a device name, plan and purchase references, activation timestamps, and anti-abuse rate-limit records. The app keeps an encrypted local activation cache so a previously activated Pro license can continue working during a temporary outage.

Gumroad processes purchase and license verification data under its own privacy policy. Cloudflare operates the license-service infrastructure and may process connection and request metadata to deliver and protect that service. Contact Gumroad or Cloudflare for information about their respective processing.

4. Updates and third-party services

The app may check GitHub for release information when its update checker runs. If you install DownGo through Microsoft Store, Microsoft handles Store installation and Store-managed updates under Microsoft's terms and privacy policy. The website's support form uses Supabase to submit support requests and is loaded with the Supabase JavaScript library from jsDelivr. GitHub, Microsoft, Supabase, jsDelivr, Gumroad, Cloudflare, and download hosts process information under their own policies when you use the related feature or service.

5. Website support requests

If you submit the support form, it collects the name, email address, request category, subject, and message you provide. The request is stored in the DownGo support database so the support team can review and respond. Your email address is used to reply to your request; the form does not automatically subscribe you to marketing email. Do not include passwords, license keys, payment-card details, or other sensitive information in a support message.

6. Retention, security, and your choices

Local app data remains on your device until you remove it. Support and license records are kept for as long as needed to provide support, operate and secure licensing, resolve disputes, and meet applicable obligations. Access to support tickets and license administration is restricted to authorized operators. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

You can control extension features and site access in your browser, disable optional browser-session cookie use, remove an imported cookies file, and choose whether to activate Pro. You may ask us to access, correct, or delete personal information associated with a support request or license by emailing contact@downgo.online. We may need to retain some records where required to operate a purchase or meet legal obligations.

7. Children and policy changes

DownGo is a general-purpose download utility and is not designed to collect personal information from children. We may update this policy when the product or its data practices change. The revised date above indicates when this page was most recently updated.

This policy describes the current product and website data flows as understood by the publisher. For questions or privacy requests, contact contact@downgo.online.
