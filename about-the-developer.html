About the Developer

My name is Nurnobi Islam, and I am from Gazipur, Bangladesh. I am a first-year Chemistry student at the National University of Bangladesh. Alongside my studies, I independently develop software and created DownGo using Python.

DownGo – Fast Download Manager is my independent Windows software project, published on the Microsoft Store under the Rafix Technologies brand. I continue learning, testing, and improving it as I balance software development with my studies.
