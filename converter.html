DownGo Pro Tool · Media Processing

Built-in Video & Audio Format Converter

Convert downloaded video files directly within DownGo. No need for sketchy online conversion websites or separate heavy encoders—convert files instantly on your local hardware.

Why Use a Local Converter?

Online video conversion sites require you to upload multi-gigabyte files to a remote server, wait for hours, and then re-download the converted file, compromising your privacy and consuming valuable bandwidth. DownGo's integrated converter processes files 100% locally on your computer with maximum speed and complete privacy.

Supported Video Formats

  • MP4 (H.264 / AAC): Maximum compatibility across smart TVs, smartphones, web players, and video editors.
  • MKV (Matroska): The industry-standard container for high-definition video, multiple audio streams, and embedded subtitles.
  • WebM (VP9 / Opus): Lightweight open web media format optimized for web streaming and HTML5 players.
  • AVI: Legacy container compatible with older media playback hardware and car systems.

How to Convert Videos in DownGo

  1. In DownGo's main download table, select any completed video file.
  2. Click Converter (🎞️) in the top toolbar (or right-click the file and select Convert Format).
  3. Select your desired target container (MP4, MKV, AVI, WebM).
  4. Click Start Conversion. DownGo converts the file in a background thread and preserves your original file intact.
