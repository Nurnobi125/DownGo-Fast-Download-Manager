Managing Download Queues, Categories & Priority

Efficient queue management is essential when handling dozens of simultaneous transfers. Learn how to pause, resume, prioritize, and automate file sorting in DownGo.

1. Reordering Tasks by Priority

In DownGo, queued items are downloaded sequentially based on your priority preferences:

  • Drag-to-Reorder (Pro): Simply click and drag any row in the main download table to reposition it higher or lower in the queue.
  • Top Priority: Items at the top of the table are given first access to active download slots when older tasks complete.

2. Simultaneous Download Limits

Running too many downloads at once can overwhelm home routers and divide your bandwidth unnecessarily:

  • Go to Settings > Downloads.
  • Set Maximum Simultaneous Downloads (recommended: 2 to 4 concurrent downloads for optimal throughput).
  • Remaining tasks will stay in the queue in pending state and start automatically as active downloads finish.

3. Categorized File Organization

DownGo includes smart file-sorting rules out of the box. As soon as a file is added, DownGo inspects its extension and places it in the appropriate folder:

  • Videos — MP4, MKV, AVI, WebM, MOV, TS, M4S
  • Music — MP3, FLAC, AAC, WAV, OGG, M4A
  • Compressed — ZIP, RAR, 7Z, TAR, GZ, ISO
  • Documents — PDF, DOCX, XLSX, PPTX, TXT
  • Programs — EXE, MSI, MSIX, APK, DMG

4. Batch Link Queueing

When downloading multiple files from a forum, archive, or asset folder, click Batch in the top toolbar. Paste a list of URLs (one per line) and click Add All to Queue. DownGo will validate the headers and queue them without opening dozens of separate popup windows.
