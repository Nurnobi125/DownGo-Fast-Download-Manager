Troubleshooting Downloads

Resolve interruptions by checking your connection, the remote server, file permissions, and disk space.

Connection timed out

Check your network and try again later. The server may be unavailable or limiting requests.

Resume failed

The server may not support resume, or the remote file may have changed.

Not enough space

Check free space on the destination drive and confirm that the folder is writable.

Checksum mismatch

Download again from the official source and compare with a trusted checksum.
