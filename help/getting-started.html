Getting Started with DownGo: Complete Setup Walkthrough

Welcome to DownGo! This quickstart guide walks you through installing the application on Windows 10 or 11, configuring multi-threaded parallel acceleration, setting up browser integration, and managing your first download queue.

Step 1: Installation

DownGo is available as a verified package on the Microsoft Store for automated updates and zero dependencies:

  1. Visit the official DownGo Microsoft Store listing.
  2. Click Install to install the verified package directly to your PC.
  3. Launch DownGo from your Windows Start Menu or taskbar.

Step 2: Choosing Your Download Folder & Smart Categories

By default, DownGo saves downloaded files to your standard Windows Downloads folder. You can customize this anytime:

  • Click Settings (⚙) in the top toolbar.
  • Under the Downloads tab, click Browse Folder to select your preferred storage drive (e.g. an external SSD or secondary HDD).
  • DownGo will automatically sort completed files into subfolders: Compressed, Documents, Music, Videos, and Programs.

Step 3: Adding Your First Download

Adding transfers is fast and flexible:

  • Direct Paste: Copy any download link and press Ctrl+V inside DownGo, or click + Add URL.
  • Batch Queue: Click Batch to paste a block of multiple URLs to queue them all at once.
  • BitTorrent: Paste a magnet link or click Torrent File to add a .torrent file.

Step 4: Installing the Browser Companion

To capture video streams and download links automatically while browsing the web:

  1. Install the official DownGo Chrome Extension from the Chrome Web Store (works on Google Chrome, Brave, and Microsoft Edge).
  2. Ensure DownGo is running on your desktop.
  3. Open the extension popup to verify that the status displays Connected (via local port 127.0.0.1:8765).
  4. Now, clicking download links or watching videos will reveal the 1-click DownGo download panel.

Step 5: Monitoring Live Progress & Taskbar

While downloads are running, you can monitor individual chunk speeds, remaining time (ETA), and resume capability. Thanks to native Windows ITaskbarList3 integration, you can also track progress from your Windows taskbar without switching windows.
