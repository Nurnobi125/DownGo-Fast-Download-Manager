Getting Started with DownGo

A checklist for installing DownGo from Microsoft Store and organizing your first download.

Open the official Microsoft Store listing linked on our download page and install DownGo from there. Start DownGo, add a file URL you are authorized to access, choose a destination folder, and monitor the transfer in the queue. Verify important downloads against the publisher checksum when available.
