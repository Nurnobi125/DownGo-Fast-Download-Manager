About the app

DownGo -Fast Download Manager

DownGo is a Windows download manager published by Rafix Technologies in the Microsoft Store Utilities & tools category.

Use DownGo to download, organize, and monitor files with pause, resume, retry, queue, browser handoff, and optional Pro tools. The Store listing displays a PEGI 3 rating and says the app offers in-app purchases.
